Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use the service, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers, and passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal and expense data within a reasonable period. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services, such as Google Fonts for typography, which may receive your IP address when loading the page. We do not share your expense data with these services. Any third party we rely on is expected to handle information in line with this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
